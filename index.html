Data notes

Output price per 1M tokens, standard tier, USD. A month's value is the price in effect that month. DeepSeek plots off-peak rates (peak is Mon to Fri 01:00 to 04:00 and 06:00 to 10:00 UTC, at 2x). Meta's values before Oct 2026 are representative host list rates (Together, DeepInfra) because Meta ran no first-party API; Muse Wonder is priced on the Meta Model API. xAI's 2025 rates (Grok 2 $10, Grok 3 and 4 $15) come from xAI's published 2025 rate card. Kimi K2 uses Moonshot's 0711 list rate; K2.5 interim pricing in early 2026 is not tracked. Qwen3.7-Max is shown at its 50% promo rate ($3.75; list $7.50). Gemini 4 Argon's $10 is an intro rate that steps to $20 on Dec 1. Anthropic's line follows the Fable tier from Jun 2026, when it became Anthropic's most capable model.

Not charted: GPT-6 Luna ($0.50), GPT-5.6 Terra ($12), Sonnet 5.5 ($10) and the Gemini 3.x Flash promo ($3.75), because mid-tier and promo models are excluded. GPT-5.6 Sol ($30) sits between GPT-5.5 and GPT-6 at an unchanged price.
