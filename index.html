Key Takeaways

  • OpenAI's flagship is the most expensive line ever charted. GPT-6 Astra hit $50 output in Sep 2026; GPT-6.2 Astra (Nov 17) cut it to $40 — OpenAI's first flagship price cut since 2024. The cheap tier collapsed in parallel: GPT-6 Luna is $0.10/$0.50, undercutting every Chinese flagship here.
  • Anthropic re-baselined its top tier. Fable 5.1 (Jun 26) took the flagship from Opus's $25 to $50; Fable 5.2 (Nov 9) cut it 20% to $40. Opus 5.5 (Sep 22) delivered the first Opus cut in years: $25 → $20 output.
  • The Chinese lines moved up, not down. DeepSeek V4 Pro jumped $0.87 → $1.98 off-peak (peak 2×) on Aug 16. GLM held $4.40 across two flagship generations. MiniMax remains the floor: $1.20 since February, unchanged through Nov.
  • Kimi made the steepest single jump: K2 held $2.20 for a year, then K3 launched at $15 in Jul 26 (7x). The successor K3.1 Pro immediately undercut K3 by a third at $10.
  • Meta rejoined the frontier in one day. Llama 4 Maverick sat at $0.85 (host list) for 18 months; Muse Wonder launched at $20 output on the Meta Model API (Oct 12) — a 23x jump, the chart's largest single move.
  • Mistral whipsawed both directions: $6 → $1.50 (Dec 25, −75%) → $7.50 (Apr 26) → $4.18 preview (Oct 26). The flagship got cheaper, then the next flagship repriced up.
  • Google's flagship got cheaper for once. Gemini 4 Argon launched at $10 intro (Oct 13) vs 3.1 Pro's $12 — but the intro expires Dec 1 and doubles to $20.
  • Four of the last six flagship launches cut the entry price. GPT-6.2 Astra (−20%), Fable 5.2 (−20%), K3.1 Pro (−33%), Gemini 4 Argon (intro $10 vs $12). The two raises: DeepSeek (Aug restructure) and Meta (Muse Wonder).
